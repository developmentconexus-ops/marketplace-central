# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal marketplace operators responsible for Mercado Livre orders, product linkage, data-quality review, profitability analysis, and audited manual corrections. They work in a dense operational cockpit and need to distinguish actionable facts from missing or unproven data quickly.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy, server-backed orders and margin workspace that makes provider state, source timestamps, link quality, financial inputs, realization, and audit evidence visible without guessing. Success means an operator can understand an order's operational and profitability state, identify blockers, and take bounded audited action.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, operational, evidence-first. The interface should feel calm under pressure, precise about uncertainty, and confident without hiding incomplete data.

## Anti-references

- Consumer marketplace storefronts that prioritize promotion over operational truth.
- Generic SaaS dashboards that decorate metrics but obscure source, quality, or actionability.
- Interfaces that collapse unknown values into zero, imply success without evidence, or use color as the only state signal.
- Novel controls or ornamental motion that slow repeated operator workflows.

## Design Principles

- Make state and evidence legible before inviting action.
- Preserve uncertainty explicitly; never manufacture completeness.
- Keep repeated workflows familiar, dense, and consistent with the existing cockpit.
- Separate operational state from data quality so operators know what happened and what is missing.
- Prefer durable, accessible semantics over decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA behavior: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, semantic labels, non-color state text, readable contrast, reduced-motion respect, and usable desktop/mobile layouts. Loading, error, empty, incomplete, cancelled, and unknown states must remain understandable without relying on color alone.
