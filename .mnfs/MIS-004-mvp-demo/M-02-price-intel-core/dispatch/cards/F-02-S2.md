- **`tenant_id` on EVERY table**, first PK/index component. Every future query filters `tenant_id = $1`.
- Field sets mirror the S1 domain structs:
  - snapshots: scope, ref_id, source, price (NULLABLE), currency, observed_at, fetched_at, expires_at, status, failure_reason (NULLABLE), request_id. **Idempotency uniqueness** on `(tenant_id, scope, ref_id, source, fetched_at)` (per F-02-S3).

  - competitive_signals: listing_id, our_price, winner_price (NULLABLE), target_price (NULLABLE), position_rank (NULLABLE), position_total (NULLABLE), fetched_at.
  - aggregates: product_id, median (NULLABLE), min_valid (NULLABLE), n_offers, n_sellers, source, fetched_at, computed_at, status.
- Nullable columns are genuinely `NULL`-able (ADR-17: unknown ≠ zero — no `DEFAULT 0`, no `NOT NULL DEFAULT ''` on unknowns). Enum-like columns may use CHECK constraints matching the exact uppercase value sets in evidence.go.
- Forward-only: no down migrations. Applied lexicographically via `embed.FS` (`migrations/source.go`).
