     reader.go:184  rows, collisions := index.candidateRows(input), index.collisions
     reader.go:203  r.candidate(int(id), row, collisions, source)
     reader.go:568  collisions[*ean] >= 2  ->  ambiguity flag
     The collision map comes from MirrorEANCollisionCounts, a SQL aggregate over
     the whole mirror (mirror_query_repository.go:166) that knows nothing about
     the policy. If two rows share an EAN and the rule cuts one, the SURVIVOR is
     still flagged ambiguous. Under the D-121 policy ambiguous goes to manual
     confirmation instead of auto-approving, so cutting junk makes auto-linking
     WORSE for that product -- the opposite of the chip's purpose.
     Note the inconsistency inside one function: the OTHER ambiguity mechanism
     (reader.go:219, len(results) > 1) is computed correctly AFTER the cut.
     Fixing it properly means making the collision count policy-aware, which is
     mirror-side SQL filtering = S7's seam. Proposed: S7 carries it. Not fixed
     here.
