  • Family exists by distinct D4 read/coverage + consumer claim, not topic name.
  • No fabricated SourceInstance/Sankhya callback seam.
CANONICAL

Custody and acknowledgement

  • Protocol-invalid → reject/no custody.
  • Non-admitted → explicit terminal non-processing.
  • Admitted + attributed → recoverable Organization custody.
  • Quarantine only for plausible pending legitimate bind.
  • ACK needs custody, bounded quarantine or terminal disposition; never business success.
CANONICAL

Authorization ceremony

  • Current human + eligibility + Membership + portfolio.manage + exact Installation.
  • Not a 96th Product operation.
  • Opaque single-use state + transaction-bound anti-injection; PKCE for current ML lane where supported.
  • Selling-account/admin proof; same-seller only.
  • Generation-safe activation; single-use refresh serialized by correctness.

Deactivation and history

Deactivation removes business participation, not retained namespace attribution or evidence recovery. Same-seller technical reauthorization may restore read capability without reactivation or write/publication authority.

Product/protocol separation

Provider callback, notification, OAuth and internal acquisition are outside Product roots, Product OpenAPI and SDK. Exact technical host/prefix is deferred, but collision with /access-context or Organization roots is forbidden.
